    Good to know

    Where do the prices come from?

    Wizz Air prices are read from Wizz Air’s own fare calendar for every route from Chișinău, twice a day. When other airlines are switched on, their prices come from Aviasales and are prices other travellers saw recently — those are marked “Recent price”.

    Why can the price differ on the airline’s website?

    Fares change all the time and seats sell out. We show the lowest fare when we last checked (see “updated” at the top). Always confirm the final price on the booking page.

    What does the price include?

    It’s the cheapest return fare for one adult. For Wizz Air that’s the Basic fare with one small personal item under the seat (40 × 30 × 20 cm). Cabin trolleys, checked bags and seats cost extra.

    Are the flight times exact?

    Departure times are real. Arrival times and flight durations are estimated from the distance and the time zones at each airport, and marked with “≈”.

    How do the Telegram alerts work?

    Twice a day the scanner looks for destinations that newly have a return trip under your price limit, or got at least €1 cheaper, and sends them to your Telegram — never the same deal twice.

    Can I search for more than one traveller?

    Yes — pick the number of adults in the search. Prices stay per person and the total for everyone is shown next to them. Child fares may differ on the airline’s site.
